The Question We Heard

National AI Commission — commissioner framing

The Commission’s Question

  • AI is already in healthcare
  • Support is strong — and conditional
  • People asked how it can be used safely, fairly and transparently
Open warmly. You are among colleagues who did the work. Acknowledge Chairs Denniston and Hughes and the other Working Group leads in the room.

Designed for a Different Era

Static Products, Evolving Systems

  • Much of today’s framework assumes a product assessed at a point in time
  • AI-enabled products can iterate, drift and perform differently by setting
  • A one-off pre-market snapshot is not enough
One concrete example if time allows: ambient documentation tools that combine transcription with clinical decision support — regulated and unregulated functions in one product. That is why function-based approaches matter. Do not linger; the MHRA team will show practical pathways.

What the Technology Working Group Saw

Collaboration — and Confusion

  • An enthusiasm across government, regulators, industry and the NHS to work together
  • A landscape where it is often unclear who is in charge
  • Gaps where no one acts; overlaps where everyone does
This is your distinctive contribution as Technology WG Chair. Personal but collegiate: “we” not “they.” The confusion about who is in charge is exactly why Principle 2 exists — flag it lightly, do not deliver that chapter now.

Proportionate Lifecycle Regulation

What Chapter 1 Proposes

  • Clear qualification and classification — when is a product a medical device?
  • Risk-proportionate oversight — including function-based regulation
  • Evidence and change managed across the lifecycle, not only at launch
  • Staged routes to market, sandboxes and stronger post-market learning
You are teeing up the next speakers. Mention AI Airlock by name — it is in the room. Sandboxes and staged authorisations bridge to industry examples on the panel.

Safe, Fast and Trusted

Not a False Trade-off

  • Safe — protecting patients and managing risk across the lifecycle
  • Fast — responsible innovation and timely access to benefit
  • Trusted — confidence for patients, professionals, providers and developers
Last minute before handover: one sentence on international harmonisation and UK leadership if the room cares about investment — do not make it a trade pitch.
