Communication Bandwidth

  • Human communication: walking pace (2000 bits/minute)
  • Machine communication: light speed (billions of bits/second)
  • Our sharing walks, machine sharing …

From Marconi to AI

  • Marconi made communication wireless and scalable
  • AI makes prediction and decision scalable
  • The question shifts from “can we transmit?” to “what do we transmit and trust?”

Security and Creativity

  • Security: where do we place trust when systems act at machine speed?
  • Creativity: what remains uniquely human when machines can mimic style?
  • Culture is the interface: institutions, norms, and art shape adoption

For sale: baby shoes, never worn
Consider the six-word novel, apocryphally credited to Ernest Hemingway, “For sale: baby shoes, never worn.” To understand what that means to a human, you need a great deal of additional context. Context that is not directly accessible to a machine that has not got both the evolved and contextual understanding of our own condition to realize both the implication of the advert and what that implication means emotionally to the previous owner.
